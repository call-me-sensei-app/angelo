# Angelo Trademark Policy

Angelo is open source software: its source code is licensed under the GNU
General Public License (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [COPYING](COPYING)). This
policy is about something different, the **Angelo name and logos**, which tell
people that a copy of Angelo comes from us. It explains how you may use them.

## Our marks

The following are trademarks of **Hyperbond Studio Pte. Ltd.** ("we"):

- the name **Angelo**, when used for software, services or websites related to
  3D creation;
- the **Angelo logo**: the teal raven robot head;
- the **Angelo wordmark**: the word Angelo with its faceted, triangular "A".

The artwork is in [release/branding/angelo/](release/branding/angelo/) and in
the files generated from it (application and document icons, the splash
screen, installer artwork and interface glyphs). This policy applies to the
marks wherever they appear, whether or not a file is annotated with
`LicenseRef-Angelo-Trademarks`.

## The GPL and the marks

The GPL licenses the copyright in Angelo's source code. It does not grant any
right to use our trademarks, and the marks are not licensed under the GPL.
Nothing in this policy limits what the GPL lets you do with the code: you may
study, change and share it under the GPL's terms. Only the name and logos are
restricted, so that people can tell an official Angelo from a modified one.

## Uses that need no permission

You may, without asking us:

- **Refer to Angelo.** Use the name to say truthfully what something is or works
  with, for example "a tutorial for Angelo", "an add-on that works with Angelo"
  or "based on Angelo". This includes news reports, reviews, teaching material,
  research papers and commentary.
- **Redistribute official builds unmodified.** Share an installer or app that we
  published, exactly as we published it, with its name and logos intact. Do not
  bundle it in a way that suggests we made or endorse the bundle.
- **Build it for yourself.** Build Angelo from source for your own development
  and testing, keeping the name and logos, as long as you do not distribute that
  build.
- **Make other fair use** of the marks that applicable law allows.

When you use the name, do not suggest that we sponsor, endorse or are affiliated
with you or your product, and do not use the logos in place of your own.

## Modified builds and forks

If you distribute a build made from source that differs from one of our
releases, including a fork, you must give it a **different name and logo** that
are not confusingly similar to ours:

- Replace the Angelo name and artwork in the application, its icons, splash
  screen, installers, window titles and documentation. The product name,
  identifiers and links are defined once in
  [scripts/modules/angelo_identity.json](scripts/modules/angelo_identity.json),
  and the artwork in [release/branding/angelo/](release/branding/angelo/).
- You may still describe your build truthfully in text, for example "based on
  Angelo", but not as its name or with our logos.

## Uses that need our permission

Ask us before you:

- use a mark, or something confusingly similar, in the name of a product,
  service, company, domain name or social media account;
- put the marks on merchandise or other goods;
- change the logos, combine them with other marks, or use them in your own
  branding;
- use the marks in any way not described above.
